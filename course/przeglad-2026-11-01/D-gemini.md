7. Major (Technical — SAR Floods) — Unrealistic < 2× completion gate and inconsistent sensitivity table specification in Lab 4
- Location: s4-lesson-401.intro.html:31, s4-lesson-401-p116.html:47-64, s4-assign-431.intro.html:25-32, L04_Floods.md:332
- What is wrong: p116 (line 64) and s4-assign-431.intro.html (line 32) state "You are done when… the area changes by less than a factor of two between the thresholds 1.15 and 1.40." In COPERNICUS/S1_GRD, a dB ratio of 1.15 around −12 dB represents only a ~1.8 dB drop (well within normal SAR speckle and soil-moisture fluctuation), whereas 1.40 is a ~4.8 dB drop; across real AOIs, flooded area routinely varies by 3×–10× between 1.15 and 1.40, making students think their working script is broken. In addition, s4- lesson-401.intro.html and L04_Floods.md ask for a 1D table (1.15 / 1.25 / 1.40), whereas p116 and s4-assign-431.intro.html demand a 3 × 3 table (threshold × filter radius 30/50/100 m).
- Suggested fix: Reword the < 2× rule from a pass/fail completion check to an interpretation guide (matching quiz question L4-12 feedback), and align s4-lesson-401. intro.html and L04_Floods.md on the 3 × 3 table.
8. Major (Didactic & Technical — ML Validation) — Polygon pixel sampling in Lab 3 inflates accuracy via spatial autocorrelation
- Location: s3-lesson-396-p108.html:26-49, L03_ML_Classification.md:214-230
- What is wrong: Students draw polygons of 50–500 pixels each, extract all pixels via sampleRegions, and randomly split pixels 70/30 (randomColumn). Because test pixels come from inside the same homogeneous polygons 10 m away from training pixels, spatial autocorrelation inflates test accuracy (>95%) and contradicts the independent validation principles taught in Lecture 8.
- Suggested fix: Instruct students either to collect dispersed point samples (50–100 points per class) or to add randomColumn to trainingSamples (splitting polygons 70/30) before running sampleRegions.
