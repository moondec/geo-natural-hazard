1. Unsupervised Classification - unsupervised learning.

It is used when the human expert has no knowledge of the classes in the data. He does not know the patterns that can be used to mark such classes. ML can find patterns and help to understand the processes and relationships hidden in the data. However, be careful because the computer can find patterns without cause and effect relationship. In such a situation, it may lead to erroneous conclusions.
