This lesson is a short version of "Flood mapping approaches" course by Jannik Jänichen and Kai Heckel at https://www.sentinel-hub.com/explore/education/ platform

  • Multispectral tools to monitor floods.
  • Learn about water body mapping indices.
  • Understand the relation between water bodies and SAR backscatter.
  • Gain understanding of InSAR for flood mapping.

References: Du, Y., Zhang, Y., Ling, F., Wang, Q., Li, W. & Li, X. (2016). Water Bodies’ Mapping from Sentinel-2 Imagery with Modified Normalized Difference Water Index at 10-m Spatial Resolution Produced by Sharpening the SWIR Band. Remote Sensing, 8,354, https://doi.org/10.3390/rs8040354.
