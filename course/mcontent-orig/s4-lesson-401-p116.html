ICEYE i Finish-Polish company produces SAR satellites and sells access to data collected from a constellation of over 50 satellites. This allows for near-real-time monitoring of surface changes in all atmospheric conditions with a spatial resolution of 25 cm. The revisit time is 2 hours, and in the future, it will be 30 minutes.
