One index for the presence or absence of water would be the Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI), defined as:

NDWI = (Green - NIR)/(Green + NIR)

It is a normalized index, so its values lay between -1 for no water and +1 for the presence of water.

However, NDWI cannot efficiently suppress the signal noise from the land cover features of built-up areas. 

Water bodies have a stronger absorbability in the SWIR band than in the NIR band, and the built-up class has greater radiation in the SWIR band than in the NIR band. Based on this finding the Modified Normalized Difference Water Index was proposed, which is defined as:


MNDWI = (Green - SWIR)/(Green + SWIR)

The Modified Normalized Difference Water Index (MNDWI). Source: Huang et al. 2018.

Compared to the NDWI, water bodies have greater positive values in the MNDWI because water bodies absorb more SWIR light than NIR light.
