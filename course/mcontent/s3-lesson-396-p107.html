Optional, but this is where the accuracy goes from "acceptable" to "good". This page lists the improvements shown in the lecture and gives three ready scripts that apply them. Use what you need to raise the accuracy of your own classification before writing the report.

Six ways to improve a classification

  1. Apply cloud masking per pixel before taking the median: mask the SCL classes 3, 8, 9, 10 (cloud shadow, clouds, cirrus) or, better, use Cloud Score+ (GOOGLE/CLOUD_SCORE_PLUS/V1/S2_HARMONIZED, keep pixels with cs_cdf ≥ 0.6). Do not rely on QA60 for scenes from 25 Jan 2022 to 28 Feb 2024 – the band is unpopulated there. Lesson 2, step 5 (Clouds first) explains the three masks and the bitmask.
  2. Add spectral indices as extra bands: NDVI (vegetation), NDBI (built-up), MNDWI (water) and BSI (bare soil).
  3. Add elevation and slope from a DEM – forests and crops rarely share the same slope.
  4. Normalise the inputs to 0–1 (divide each band by its maximum). Machine learning models work best when all inputs have the same scale; it is required for SVM or neural networks and harmless for Random Forest. A more complete and robust technique is described in the End-to-End course supplement.
  5. More trees (100–200) and more, better-distributed samples.
  6. Post-processing: a majority filter (focal_mode) removes salt-and-pepper pixels.

Adding indices and terrain to the band stack

var ndvi  = composite.normalizedDifference(['B8', 'B4']).rename('ndvi');
var mndwi = composite.normalizedDifference(['B3', 'B11']).rename('mndwi');
var ndbi  = composite.normalizedDifference(['B11', 'B8']).rename('ndbi');
var dem   = ee.Image('NASA/NASADEM_HGT/001').select('elevation');
var slope = ee.Terrain.slope(dem).rename('slope');
var stack = composite.addBands([ndvi, mndwi, ndbi, dem, slope]);
// then train on stack.bandNames() instead of composite.bandNames()

You are done when…

…you can name the two inputs with the highest importance in classifier.explain() and your overall accuracy did not fall after adding them.

Common mistake

Adding the index bands to the image but still training on composite.bandNames() – the classifier then never sees them. Use stack.bandNames() and classify stack.
