Flood mapping: optical water indices and Sentinel-1 radar

Floods happen under clouds. Optical indices (NDWI, MNDWI) work on the first clear day; Sentinel-1 radar sees the peak through the clouds – calm water is dark. You run the UN-SPIDER change-detection script and learn where it fails.
