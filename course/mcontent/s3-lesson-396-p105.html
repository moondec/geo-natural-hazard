What is artificial intelligence and machine learning?

  • Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of computers to perform a task that typically requires some level of human intelligence.
  • Machine learning (ML) is a field of AI that uses statistical techniques to give computer systems the ability to "learn" from data – to improve their performance on a specific task – without being explicitly programmed.

In lab 2 you wrote the rule (NDVI > 0.4 → forest). This week you give the computer examples (pixels with a known class) and it finds the rule in all bands at once: B2…B12, NDVI, MNDWI, elevation, slope. Each pixel is one row of a table, each band one column (a feature or predictor), the class is the label.
