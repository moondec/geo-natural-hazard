Wavelengths used for flood monitoring

Water absorbs near-infrared (NIR) and short-wave infrared (SWIR) radiation almost completely and reflects only a little green and blue light, whereas vegetation and soil reflect NIR and SWIR strongly. The table lists the wavelength regions that are suitable and broadly used for flood monitoring.

The observation problem

The flood peak lasts hours to days – it often falls between two Sentinel-2 passes (5 days) and under the clouds that caused it. Optical data are good for the situation after the event and for permanent water; the peak itself is usually mapped with radar (page 5).
