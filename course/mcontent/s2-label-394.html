Administrative borders instead of a drawn polygon

Administrative borders you can download from GADM or the simplemaps.com website and upload as an asset. In Earth Engine the FAO GAUL borders are already in the catalog – here is a piece of code (ee.FeatureCollection()):

var extent_pl = ee.FeatureCollection("FAO/GAUL/2015/level0")
                  .filter(ee.Filter.eq("ADM0_NAME", "Poland")); // filter for the entry that equals the UN country name "Poland"

or an example from the End-to-End course:

var admin2 = ee.FeatureCollection("FAO/GAUL_SIMPLIFIED_500m/2015/level2");
var karnataka = admin2.filter(ee.Filter.eq("ADM1_NAME", "Karnataka"));
var visParams = {"color": "red"};

Map.addLayer(karnataka, visParams, "Karnataka Districts");

Note

The numbers 0, 1 and 2 in ADM0_NAME, ADM1_NAME, ADM2_NAME refer to the level of the administrative division (country, region, district). A named unit makes your homework reproducible; use .geometry() of the filtered collection as roi.
