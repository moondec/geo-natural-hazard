Step 3: load a Sentinel-2 image collection into your script. At the end you know where to find a collection ID in the Data Catalog and have a first layer on the map.

  1. Open the Data Catalog. Search the Earth Engine Data Catalog for Sentinel-2. Every dataset page shows its ImageCollection ID.
  2. Choose the surface-reflectance collection. Open Sentinel-2 MSI, Level-2A, harmonized; its ID is COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED. This is the collection we use for indices and classification in the whole course.
  3. Copy the snippet. In the section Explore in Earth Engine click Open in Code Editor or copy the code; it loads, filters and visualizes the collection.
  4. Run it and look at the map and at the Console. Then press Next for a short quiz.

Level-1C or Level-2A?

COPERNICUS/S2_HARMONIZED is Level-1C (top-of-atmosphere reflectance, archive from 2015). COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED is Level-2A (surface reflectance, atmospherically corrected, plus the SCL band). Reflectance is stored as integer × 10 000; a value of 2500 means 0.25.
