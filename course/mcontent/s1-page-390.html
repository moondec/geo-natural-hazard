Homework after Lecture 1 (9.11). Before Lab 1 on 16.11 you need two working accounts and about one hour of videos. At the end of this page you know exactly what to click and what to bring to the lab.

Do it now – it takes time

  1. Create a free CDSE account. Go to dataspace.copernicus.eu, register, then log in to Copernicus Browser. Copernicus Browser replaces EO Browser (retired in March 2026).
  2. Register for Google Earth Engine. Follow How to get access to Earth Engine: sign in with a Google account, create a Google Cloud project and register it for noncommercial (education) use. Registration for noncommercial use is enabled immediately, but creating the Cloud project, enabling the Earth Engine API and the eligibility questionnaire take 20–30 minutes and sometimes fail – do it before Friday. Noncommercial projects are periodically re-verified, so keep the e-mails from Google.
  3. Open the Code Editor once. Visit code.earthengine.google.com; if it opens without an error, your project is registered.
  4. Watch the webinars below. The first three are required; the rest are optional.
  5. Pick one hazard event you are curious about (place and approximate date) and bring it to the lab.

Webinars to watch

  • A short guide to Copernicus Browser (about 10 min) – required.
  • Introduction to the CDSE website and Copernicus Browser – required.
  • Module 1: Earth Engine Basics – End-to-End GEE (Spatial Thoughts) – required; watch at least the first part (Code Editor, hello world).
  • A Short Course on Earth Observation Methods and Data – optional.
  • FU Berlin GEO-IT online seminar 7 – optional.
  • Browse Earth Engine resources for workshops – optional.
  • Archive: Sentinel Hub webinars – recorded for EO Browser; the tools work almost identically in Copernicus Browser.

Supplementary material

  • NASA ARSET – Fundamentals of Remote Sensing (live webinars, registration required).

Milestone M1 (16.11): accounts ready + first script

In Lab 1 you will log in to Copernicus Browser, open the Code Editor and run your first Earth Engine script. Both accounts must work before the lab starts.

You are done when

Copernicus Browser shows a Sentinel-2 image after you log in, the Code Editor opens without a registration warning, and you have watched the three required videos.

Problems with registration?

Write on the course forum before Friday, not on Monday morning. Earth Engine registration has several steps that cannot be fixed during the lab.
