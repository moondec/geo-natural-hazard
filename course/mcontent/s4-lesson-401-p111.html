Which optical satellites deliver the images for flood mapping. After this page you know what resolution and revisit time to expect from Landsat and Sentinel-2 and which collection to open in GEE.

Landsat and Sentinel-2

Landsat 8/9 (and the former Landsat satellites, back to the 1970s) and the Sentinel-2 satellites deliver a huge data archive with high spatial resolution and a short revisit time – the two properties that matter for floods.

Sentinel-1 A/C/DC-band SAR10 m (IW GRD)6 days over EuropeCOPERNICUS/S1_GRD

Landsat 7 was retired in 2025; Sentinel-2C joined the constellation in 2024. All of these data are free and open in the Copernicus Browser and in Earth Engine.
