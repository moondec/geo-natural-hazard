Geoinformation and Spatial Management · winter semester

From satellite pixels to hazard maps.

Seven modules, one recipe: images before and after the event, an index or a radar echo, a threshold, a map with hectares and an honest statement of its uncertainty – for floods, landslides and wildfires, in Google Earth Engine, Copernicus Browser, Python and R.

How to use this page

Each module below has the same layout: Milestone (what you hand in and when), Your path (the order of lecture, lesson, lab and homework) and the materials. Deadlines: the milestone of module N is due at the start of lab N+1; the exact dates and times are on the assignment pages and in the course calendar. The capstone date is set in lecture 8.
