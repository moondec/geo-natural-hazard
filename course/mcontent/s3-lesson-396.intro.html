Lab 3. In this lesson you classify the land cover of your own area twice – with a supervised Random Forest and with unsupervised k-means – and you measure how good each result is with a confusion matrix. At the end you will have two classified maps, two accuracy tables and the material for the classification report (milestone M3).

How to go through the lesson

  1. ML algorithms – what supervised, unsupervised and regression mean; the two algorithms used in class.
  2. Supervised Classification – training polygons, Random Forest, accuracy assessment (the main exercise).
  3. Unsupervised Classification – k-means on the same composite, naming the clusters, accuracy.
  4. Improving the Classification – cloud mask, indices, elevation, normalisation; three ready scripts.
  5. Landslides and wildfire – two advanced examples that preview labs 5 and 6.

Milestone M3 – classification report (due at the start of lab 4)

The homework assignment below this lesson collects the report: both maps, both confusion matrices (overall accuracy and kappa), the justified number of classes, one paragraph of comparison and links to both scripts.
