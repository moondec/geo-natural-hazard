Milestone M3 – the classification report (lab 3, 30.11). Submit one .pdf or .docx file with the maps, the accuracy tables and the links to both scripts from the lesson Classifiers.

What to submit

  1. Select any area (covering 60–100 km²) with different surface types.
  2. Based on Sentinel-2 images, use a supervised (Random Forest) algorithm and make a surface classification on the basis of your own FeatureCollection (put geo-points or polygons on the map and teach the model the surface characteristics).
  3. Based on Sentinel-2 images, determine the types of surfaces using an unsupervised algorithm (k-means). Use the same area of interest (AOI) for both classifications.
  4. For both classifiers give the confusion matrix, the overall accuracy and kappa (for k-means after relabelling the clusters to your classes).
  5. Justify briefly the selected number of classes and in one paragraph describe your conclusions about both classifiers. Which one did the job better in your opinion and why? Use the accuracy numbers, not only the look of the map.
  6. Send a .pdf or .docx report on this task together with the maps and a description of your activities. Add links to both scripts.

Before you upload

Both script links open without error in a private browser window; both maps have a legend; the two confusion matrices are 4 × 4 and the accuracy numbers in the text match them.
